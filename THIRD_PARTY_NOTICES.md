`UI/media/1_portal_loop.webm` is a short clip made for the *Barotrauma: Beyond the Europa* modpack; it ships only as a demo of `<video>`.

Barotrauma is a game by FakeFish and Undertow Games; BaroUI is an unofficial mod, not affiliated with them, and ships none of the game's assets.
